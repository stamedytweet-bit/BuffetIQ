# Assumptions

This file records every assumption behind the fictional data and the rules, so that assessors can check where each number comes from. Phase 1 fills in the synthetic data section; Phase 0 records the dish settings in `config.yaml`.

## Hotel and service (Phase 0)

The prototype uses a fictional 300-room city hotel called Selat House, with breakfast at The Daybreak Room on Level 2 from 06:30 to 10:30 in sixteen 15-minute slots. The names were searched in November 2026 and no hotel or Singapore restaurant with these names was found; earlier ideas such as "Embun" were dropped because they were too close to real venues (for example *embu* at Mandarin Oriental Singapore).

## Dish settings (Phase 0, to be reviewed in Phase 1)

The eight dishes, their stations, lead times, batch sizes, pan capacities, last-cook times, costs and flags are set in `config.yaml`. The lead times (6 minutes for pancakes on the live station up to 25 minutes for nasi lemak rice) and pan capacities (1.5 kg to 5.0 kg, and 40 croissants) are chosen to match the sample screens. Costs per kilogram range from S$2.50 (rice) to S$12.00 (croissants) and were set so that the sample day's 18.6 kg of leftovers costs about S$96, as on the day-end report design. Croissants are counted in pieces on the board and converted at 60 g each for the waste report. These are placeholders until Phase 1 grounds them in sources.
